01 / THE EVENT

Build something real in 48 hours

At kickoff, every team gets a problem statement and a dataset. From there it's on you: design, build, and evaluate a data-driven solution, then present it to a panel of industry judges.

After the Day 1 kickoff you're free to work wherever you like. Optional workshops run throughout the day — data cleaning, machine learning models, and data visualization — so beginners have a clear on-ramp.

Judging is informal by design: judges rotate between teams for brief demos and Q&A, so you spend your time building, not rehearsing slides.

04 / FAQ

Good questions

Who can participate?
DataJam is built for University of Houston students. Around 80 participants across roughly 20 teams.
How big can teams be?
Up to four members per team.
Do I need machine learning experience?
No. Optional workshops on Day 1 cover data cleaning, ML models, and data visualization to get you started.
Do we have to stay on site?
After the Day 1 kickoff you can work wherever you like. Just be back and checked in by 12:00 PM on Day 2.
How does judging work?
Judges rotate between teams for short demos and Q&A instead of formal presentations.
